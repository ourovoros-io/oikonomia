## 2. What Oikonomia is

Oikonomia is a desktop application for double-entry bookkeeping. It runs
entirely on your computer and stores your financial data locally, in an
encrypted vault on your device. Oikonomia does not transmit your financial
data anywhere. The only network connection the app ever makes is a
user-initiated check for a newer app version, triggered when you click the
"Check for updates" control; nothing else in the app contacts the network.
